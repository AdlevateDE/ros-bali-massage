      Good to know

      What do I need to prepare?

      A quiet spot with room to lie down: a bed, a daybed or a clear bit of floor. The therapist brings the rest.

      Can two of us get a massage at the same time?

      Yes. Two therapists come together, so nobody has to wait. Pick 2 people in the booking.

      Will Ros come herself?

      Sometimes Ros, sometimes one of her seven colleagues. If you want a certain therapist, write the name in your message and the team will try to make it work.

      How do I pay?

      After the massage, cash in rupiah or QRIS.

      Does my hotel allow it?

      Villas and guesthouses usually do. Some resorts do not let outside therapists in. If you are unsure, ask reception before you book.

      What if I need to cancel?

      Send a WhatsApp as early as you can. There is no fee if you cancel at least 2 hours before.

      Is this a professional massage?

      Yes. Therapeutic massage only, with proper draping. Any other request ends the booking.
